MarkDaysDone helps you mark completed days, organize activities into separate calendars, and track your progress.

MarkDaysDone is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that runs in your browser and can be installed on your phone or computer. Your data is stored locally on your device, and you can export it to CSV for backup or use in Excel.

Install MarkDaysDone on iPhone

  1. Open the MarkDaysDone website in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with the upward arrow).
  3. Select Add to Home Screen.
  4. If available, enable Open as Web App.
  5. Tap Add.

The MarkDaysDone icon will appear on your Home Screen. Tap it to launch the app.

Important: Your data is stored locally. Export your calendars to CSV regularly, and keep the backup somewhere safe, especially before clearing website data or changing devices.

Install on Android

  1. Open the MarkDaysDone website in Chrome.
  2. Tap the browser menu (⋮).
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen, then follow the prompts.

Use on a computer

Open the MarkDaysDone website in a supported browser. If an Install option appears in the address bar or browser menu, use it to install the app.
